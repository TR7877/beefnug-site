BEEFNUG

A lifting log for iPhone that tells you what to load next, ranks each muscle group against your bodyweight, and keeps every number on your phone.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: email mrtitusrhodes@gmail.com. Say which iPhone and iOS version you are on, and what you were doing when it went wrong.

Common questions

Where is my data? Only on your iPhone, inside the app. There is no account and no server. It is included in your normal iPhone or iCloud backup.

How do I move my log to a new phone? Restoring your iPhone backup brings it across. You can also open Settings → Backup & Export in the app, tap Copy backup, and use Paste a backup in BEEFNUG on the new phone.

How do I get my sets into a spreadsheet? Settings → Backup & Export → Export sets CSV, then choose Save to Files, Mail or Numbers.

How do I share my program with a friend? Program → Share → Create share code. Your friend pastes the code into their own copy of BEEFNUG.

If I delete the app, is my data gone? Yes. Deleting the app deletes its data, so export a backup first.
