Privacy

Effective October 29, 2026

The short version: BEEFNUG does not collect, transmit, sell or share any of your information. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores

Your workouts, program, body measurements, profile answers (such as age, height and bodyweight) and settings are saved in the app's private storage on your device. They never leave it unless you choose to send them.

What the app sends

Nothing. BEEFNUG has no account system, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not use the internet to work.

When you choose to share

If you tap Share, Copy or Export, the app hands that text or file to iOS, and it goes wherever you send it (Messages, Mail, Files and so on). That is the only way data leaves the app, and only when you do it.

Backups

Your iPhone may include BEEFNUG's data in your own iCloud or computer backup, under Apple's terms. The developer cannot see it.

Children

BEEFNUG collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete the app, and all of its data is removed from your device.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
